# Data and Account Deletion Request

Whether you are a registered user or playing as a guest, you have the right to delete your data from **Chess 2**.

## Option 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)
The easiest way to initiate the deletion of your data (both for Registered and Guest accounts) is directly within the game. This process is automated and does not require manual verification from our support team:
1. Open the **Chess 2** application.
2. Go to the **Settings** menu.
3. Tap on **Delete Account**.
*(Note: This action will immediately start the 5-day grace period).*

## Option 2: Email Request
If you have uninstalled the app and cannot reinstall it to use the in-app deletion feature, you can request data deletion via email. 

Since we do not link accounts to email addresses, we must manually verify your identity. Please send an email to **<game4you.helper@gmail.com>** with the subject **"Account Deletion Request"** and provide **as much of the following information as possible** so we can locate your profile and confirm your ownership:
* **Username (Nickname):** (Required ONLY if you have a registered account).
* **Account Dates:** The approximate date you registered (or started playing as a guest), and the date/time of your last login or game session.
* **Device Information:** The exact device model(s) you used to register/start playing, and the device(s) you usually play on.
* **Network Information:** The IP address, city, or region you registered from, and the region you normally play from.
* **Gameplay & Social Details:** Nicknames of players you recently played against, the number of friends on your friends list, and their exact nicknames.

## Grace Period and Cancellation
Once deletion is initiated (either in-app or via email), your account enters a **5-day grace period**. During this time, your account is scheduled for deletion. 

**How to cancel:** If you change your mind, you can cancel the deletion process **only from within the game**. When you open the **Chess 2** application and attempt to connect to the server, a menu will immediately appear on your screen notifying you of the grace period. Simply tap the **"Cancel Deletion"** button to abort the deletion and restore your account (tapping "Exit" will close the app without canceling). For security reasons and to guarantee your request is processed before the timer expires, cancellations can only be made directly within the app. We do not accept cancellation requests via email.

If the process is not canceled within these 5 days, all your identifying data, statistics, and virtual currency will be permanently removed from our active servers.
