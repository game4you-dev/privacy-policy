# Account Deletion Request

If you wish to delete your **Chess 2** account and all associated data, you can do so directly within the app (Settings -> Delete Account) or by submitting a request via email.

## How to request deletion via email:
Since we do not link accounts to email addresses, we need to verify your identity. Please send an email to **<game4you.helper@gmail.com>** with the subject **"Account Deletion Request"** and include the following information:
* Your exact Username (Nickname) in the game.
* The approximate date you created the account.
* Any other details that can prove your ownership (e.g., your IP region or device model).

**Grace Period:** Once verified, your account will enter a 5-day grace period. If you do not cancel the request, all your identifying data, statistics, and virtual currency will be permanently deleted from our active servers after 5 days.
