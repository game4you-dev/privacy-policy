## Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

### Types of Data Collected

#### Data Provided by You (Optional Registration & Login)
You can use the Application as a guest without providing any personal information. However, if You choose to optionally register an account to save your progress permanently, We collect the following:
* **Username (Nickname)**
* **Password** (For Your security, Your password is cryptographically hashed on Your device using BCrypt before being transmitted. We never see or store Your plain-text password).
* **Account Security & Support Data:** During registration and subsequent account logins, Your device sends its `ANDROID_ID`, device model, Android operating system version, and IP address to our servers, where this information is securely encrypted and stored. This data is collected exclusively for security purposes (such as verifying the country of login to prevent fraud) and to allow our support team to manually verify Your identity and account ownership if You lose access and contact us for assistance at **game4you.helper@gmail.com**.

#### Data Collected Automatically (Including Guest Users)
When You launch or use the Application, We automatically collect certain information to create Your profile and ensure the game functions correctly:
* **Anonymous Identifiers:** A unique, client-generated UUID used to identify Your guest account.
* **Game Statistics & Match History:** As You play, our servers automatically generate and store Your ELO ratings, win/loss/draw records for various game modes, and detailed match histories (including move sequences and timestamps). This allows You to review Your past games and track Your progress.
* **In-Game Inventory & Progression:** We securely store Your in-game virtual currency (chips), unlocked and selected cosmetics (such as avatars and figure sets), inventory items, and VIP subscription status.
* **Player Conduct Metrics:** We maintain an automated reputation system that adjusts Your score based on in-game behavior (such as abandoning matches, failing to accept games, or disconnecting) to ensure a smooth and fair environment for all players.

#### Device Backups
Because the Application supports standard Android backup features, your device may automatically back up local app data and settings to your personal Google Drive account. This process is managed entirely by your device's operating system and Google, subject to their respective privacy policies.

#### Payment Information
If You choose to make an In-app Purchase, the transaction is handled entirely by the respective app store provider (e.g., Google Play). We do not collect, process, or store Your credit card details or payment information on our servers. We only receive a secure token to verify that a purchase was successful and to grant You the respective in-game items.

#### Third-Party Services
Our Application uses third-party services that have their own privacy policies. For information handled by Google Play Billing and Google Cloud Platform, please refer to the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Use of Your Personal Data
The Company may use Personal Data for the following purposes:
* **To provide and maintain our Service:** including calculating ELO ratings across various modes, storing match history for Your review, and facilitating Multiplayer matchmaking.
* **To manage Your Account:** managing Your registration, inventory, in-game currencies, and VIP functionalities.
* **To process In-app Purchases:** to verify transactions and deliver in-game items or subscriptions.
* **To ensure fair matchmaking and rating:** utilizing automated analysis of gameplay patterns to protect the integrity of the ELO system. If unfair play is detected, match results may be invalidated.
* **To secure and recover Your Account:** utilizing server-side encryption of your device and IP data to prevent fraud and to manually verify Your identity in case of account loss.
* **To contact You:** regarding security updates, account issues, or customer support.

### Retention and Deletion of Your Personal Data
The Company will retain Your Personal Data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy.

**Account Deletion:**
You have the right to delete Your Account and the Personal Data We have collected about You. You can request account deletion directly within the Application's settings menu (Settings -> Delete Account), or by contacting us with a deletion request at **game4you.helper@gmail.com**.
*Note: If requesting deletion via email, because We do not link accounts to email addresses, You must include specific account details in Your request (such as Your username, device information, IP region, or recent match history) so We can verify Your identity. If the provided information is insufficient, We reserve the right to request additional details to confirm You are the true owner of the account.*

**Grace Period:** When You initiate account deletion, Your account enters a 5-day grace period. During these 5 days, You can cancel the deletion process. If not canceled, after 5 days, Your identifying data, stats, inventory, and associated in-game currency will be permanently removed from our active servers, except where retention is strictly necessary to comply with legal obligations.

### Transfer of Your Personal Data

We utilize strong encryption (such as BCrypt for passwords) and encrypt sensitive security data (like IP and `ANDROID_ID`) in our databases. While We strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect Your Personal Data, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and We cannot guarantee its absolute security.
